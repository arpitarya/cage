# Proposal — speak OTel GenAI on the way out

**PICKED UP 2026-08-01** — [handoff](v0.39-otel-export.handoff.md) ·
[prompt](v0.39-otel-export.prompt.md). **Review finding that changed the design:** this
proposal called the conventions "the enterprise lingua franca". They are **pre-stable** —
as of semconv v1.42.0 (June 2026) `gen_ai.*` lives in a dedicated repo, has no 1.0, is
labelled *Development*, and names can still change. That collides with cage's
determinism law, so the build **pins and stamps the targeted semconv version** and
treats a spec bump as a deliberate, changelog'd change (the `prices_version` discipline).

**Two other plays were declined and removed** (Arpit, 2026-08-01): a `uvx` instant-trial
push and ccusage interop. The product priority is **graphify end-to-end, then a paved
road for more tools** — [tool-integration-contract.md](../proposals/tool-integration-contract.md).
This one remains parked on its own merits; nobody has accepted it either.

**Research context (2026-08):** ~10% of orgs can prove agent ROI, and those that can
attribute spend **to the commits that produced it**. Tools like ccusage (4.8k★) serve
the *capture* half at scale and do none of the *attribution* half — cage's moat is
exactly the half they don't have. That finding survives the two declined plays and
still informs the queue below.

OTel's GenAI semantic conventions (`gen_ai.client.token.usage` etc.) are becoming the
enterprise lingua franca. Cage should **export** it:

- `cage data export --otel` mapping calls → GenAI-conformant attributes. One-way
  REPORTING like CSV (never an import source); deterministic; stdlib JSON.
- Positioning: the $0 local ledger that feeds the enterprise stack instead of competing
  with it — Langfuse/Helicone/etc. become destinations, not rivals.
- Small: a serializer over existing rows. **Now specced** (was: not scheduled) — it matters for the
  team/enterprise story, not the individual one, and the graphify track comes first.

## What the research says about the existing queue

- **HR1 ask #1 (tokens per commit) is market-validated language** — "spend attributed
  to the commits that produced it" is how the orgs that *can* prove ROI describe it.
  Raise it: build after ADOPT.
- **ADOPT is timely** — adoption telemetry is an active research area (AISI's 177k MCP
  tool study); no product ships a per-agent adoption view. Keep high.
- **The honesty story is content** — "our savings tool caught its own savings claim
  being wrong" (the gross-vs-net finding) is a Show-HN-grade post the 90%-can't-prove
  stat sets up perfectly. Marketing, not code; costs a draft.

## Deliberately not proposed

Becoming a proxy/observability platform (Helicone's lane — infra, network, not $0);
per-user enterprise attribution (the Analytics-API lane — cage is per-commit/per-tool,
a different and defensible axis); real-time dashboards (determinism law: cage derives
from a ledger, it does not stream).
