## The one thing that must not go wrong

Cage has **two** unrelated "human"s:

- **Tier-1 human axis** — `human.py`, `humanview.py`, `trend.py`, `attention.py`,
  `gap_ms`, `"minutes"`. **This is what you are removing.**
- **Provenance `origin="human"`** — authorship, in `origin.py` / `originrecord.py` /
  `verifycmd.py`, with `ORIGINS` in `schema.py`. **Do not touch it.**

A blind `grep -rl human cage/` hits 41 modules and most are the wrong axis or a
false positive. Read each hit before editing it.

## Approach

1. **Explore and map before deleting.** Produce the change-map first: every file, what
   goes, what stays. Include the ones the handoff may have missed — it was written
   from a survey, not a full read.
2. **Delete the dedicated modules and surfaces**, then follow the compile/test errors
   outward. Do not try to predict every call site.
3. **Then the substrate** — `gap_ms`, `"minutes"`, `[human.*]`, `IDLE_CAP_MINUTES`.
   This is the contract change; update `PLAN.md` §3/§4.10 in the same change.
4. **Then the wiring migration** — `verbmap.REMOVED` entries, and sweep `justfile`,
   `install.sh`, `tools/dummyrepo`, the skill/steering assets.
5. **Verify against an old ledger.** Construct one with `gap_ms` and a `minutes`
   receipt, and prove every read path survives it. This is the regression most likely
   to escape.

## Hard constraints

- **Never break provenance.** `cage authorship origin|verify` must work identically.
- **Old ledgers must still read.** Append-only means they are never rewritten.
- **`cage human` must print a direction**, never exit 1 silently.
- Fail-open write paths stay fail-open; typed `CageError` only at the CLI boundary.
- stdlib only. Do not touch `work/regression/**`. No commits in `cage`.
- **No `# v2:` stubs, no commented-out code, no "keep for the rebuild" leftovers.**
  The rebuild gets a proposal doc after the release. Delete cleanly.

## Decide and state (not pre-decided)

An existing `unit="minutes"` receipt has no USD route once `human.py` is gone. Choose:
**skip it from money views with a visible footnote**, or **price it `$0`**. Either is
defensible; silently dropping it from a total is not. State the choice in the handback
