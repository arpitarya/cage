## What this run observed

In one line: *the meter works, the receipt/attribution engine has no real data,
and there is no log trail to explain why.* Cage captured **spend** well and
**savings** essentially not at all, and its own capture-health signal marked
three of four agents as not captured while their rows sat in the ledger.

both `2026-07-22-capture-report.json` and the per-lab snapshots.
